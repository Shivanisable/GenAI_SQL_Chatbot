# Suggested Questions

Use these questions with the bundled `sample_orders.csv` dataset.

1. How many records are in this dataset?
2. Show the first 5 rows.
3. How many records are there for each product?
4. What is the total sales?
5. What are the top 5 products by total sales?
6. What is the average sales for each product?
7. Which region has the highest total sales?
8. What is the total quantity sold for each product?
9. What are the sales by customer?
10. Show all orders where sales are greater than 100000.

## For a Different Dataset

Replace `product`, `sales`, `region`, `customer`, or `quantity` with the column names displayed in the terminal under **Database Schema**. The terminal application also creates dataset-aware suggestions automatically at startup.
